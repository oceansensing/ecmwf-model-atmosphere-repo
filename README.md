# ecmwf-model-atmosphere-repo

ECMWF's open forecast data, the atmosphere and the waves, as gridded data at
0.25° for the oceansensing.org ocean map: the 10 m wind, the 2 m and 850 hPa
air temperatures, the total cloud cover and the 500 hPa heights from the IFS,
and ECWAM's significant wave height — the step nearest the clock from the
newest run. These products were published by `realtime-data-repo` until
2026-11-09, under the same names.

Updated hourly; ECMWF runs four times a day.

## Sources and credits

ECMWF's open data, `https://data.ecmwf.int/forecasts`: the IFS high-resolution forecast and ECWAM. CC BY 4.0, credited as ECMWF's.
